## 9. FAQ `/faq`

### Services
**How often should an air conditioner be cleaned?** Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and consider usage, dust and operating conditions. If you're unsure, ask us about your system.  
**Can cleaning fix an air conditioner that isn't cooling?** Sometimes restricted airflow is a factor, but a lack of cooling can also indicate faults that need diagnosis. Cleaning is not a guaranteed fix.  
**Do you clean ducted and cassette systems?** We can discuss these service requests; availability depends on the equipment and confirmed business capability.  
**What if I don't know my unit type?** Describe it and include photos if possible.

### Pricing
**How much does a service cost?** Pricing depends on unit type, number of systems, access, condition and work required. Request a quote for your situation.  
**Are there additional charges?** Any applicable additional work or charges should be discussed before proceeding.  
**Can I book multiple units together?** Yes, include all units in the enquiry so they can be considered in the quote.

### Booking
**Does submitting the form confirm my booking?** No. It sends a request; we confirm availability and details before scheduling.  
**Can I suggest a date and time?** Yes. Preferred times are requests until confirmed.  
**What details should I provide?** Your suburb, contact details, system type, number of units and a brief description of the issue or service needed.

### Aftercare
**Can I use the system immediately afterwards?** Follow the technician's advice and the manufacturer's instructions for your equipment and the work performed.  
**What maintenance can I do myself?** Check your unit's manual for safe filter care and routine user maintenance; leave electrical, refrigerant and complex internal work to qualified professionals.  
**What if I notice a problem after service?** Contact us with the unit details, date of service and symptoms so the team can advise on next steps.
